# Anatomical illustration credits

The five PNG medical illustrations are by **Servier Medical Art**, a service provided by Les Laboratoires Servier, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See the original [image collection](https://smart.servier.com/). Credit, source and license links appear inside the interactive figures.

SYNAPSE adds interactive labels and approximate location markers in a separate HTML/SVG composition. These additions are educational annotations, not anatomical segmentations, measured brain activation or modifications to the original PNG bytes. The lateral and sagittal plates face right; the hippocampal plate faces left. The coronal illustration locates the basal-ganglia region approximately rather than distinguishing each nucleus.

The complete neuron diagram is by **Mariana Ruiz Villarreal (LadyofHats)** and was released to the public domain by its author. [Original file and permission](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Complete_neuron_cell_diagram_en.svg). The bundled historical SVG version is unchanged. Its Schwann-cell myelination depicts PNS biology; CNS myelin is formed by oligodendrocytes, as stated in the course.

`SOURCES.json` records each exact source page, download URL, license, changes and SHA-256 digest. These illustration licenses apply to the specified assets, independently of the Python/JavaScript implementation.
