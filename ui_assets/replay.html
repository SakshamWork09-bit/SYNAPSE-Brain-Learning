What am I watching?

This is recorded activity from a calculated 100 ms experience, slowed down for inspection. Replay changes the display only. Use the app’s Step or Start controls to calculate new experiences. The task currently uses two cues and two buttons; the movement arena comes next.

The action readout compares actual neural voltages and includes exploration. Teaching feedback arrives at 35 ms. Links shown are existing connections: a strength change does not create a new link. Packets follow arrivals actually processed by the engine, including any signals carried into this window.

Neuron locations show engineered roles. Node brightness follows recorded voltage; flashes are brief display highlights after actual spikes. Link width shows magnitude with a display scale; inhibitory links are capped for readability.
